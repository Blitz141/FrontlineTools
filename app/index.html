Battlegroup Counter

Paste an enemy import code, pick a map, get a decoded ORBAT, weakness read, opposing battlegroup suggestion, map tips, and a tactical map overlay. Runs fully offline in your browser.

Decoder: @izohek/warno-deck-utils (MIT). Unit/division data from this PC's WARNO GameData (DeckSerializer, Divisions, DivisionRules, UniteDescriptor; files dated 2025-08-18). Deck unit IDs are DeckSerializer+1. Minimap images from Steam guide Competitive Maps by Jasp137 (bundled offline). Overlay positions are hand-placed approximations, not extracted capture-zone data. Not affiliated with Eugen Systems: verify imports in Armory.
