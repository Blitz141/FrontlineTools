Company of Heroes 2 · Unofficial

Faction Plan Desk

Pick your faction and a map. No enemy picker: CoH2 is too swingy for rigid matchup scripts. You get a resource map, a general timed build order, and commander ideas for that faction (with light map notes).

Minimaps and strategic point positions are copied from your local CoH2 MPScenarios.sga / MPXP1Scenarios.sga .info and _mm_low.tga files (read-only). Build orders and commander blurbs are curated from common 1v1 fundamentals, not invented unit stats. Commander file ids come from the game AttribArchive commander RGDs. Not affiliated with Relic Entertainment.
